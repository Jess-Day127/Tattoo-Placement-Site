GOOD TO KNOW

Before you get inked

The 3 things everyone asks us. Short answers, no fluff.

Free • No sign-up • 1 minute

Is this 100% accurate?

No - it's a preview. Skin tone, body curve, and artist style will change it. Use it to check placement, size, and vibe, not as a final stencil.

Do I need an account?

No. Upload, remove the background, try it on, done. Nothing is saved unless you save it yourself.

Does it use AI to design tattoos?

No. We don't generate designs. You bring real art - yours or flash from tattoo artists.

What about pain?

Use the Pain Chart in Try-On. Bony / thin-skin spots hurt more - ribs, feet, inner arm. Fleshy spots like outer arm / thigh are easier for a first tattoo.

This is a virtual preview. Results may vary.
